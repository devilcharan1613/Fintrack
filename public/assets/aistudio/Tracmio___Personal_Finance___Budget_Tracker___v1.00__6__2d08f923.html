Tracmio Master Feature & Button Guide
Complete comprehensive manual explaining every button, screen, and feature

Tracmio is 100% offline-first. Your financial data, accounts, custom trackers, and receipts never leave your device. Here is an exhaustive guide detailing every button and function in the app.

1. How Saved Money is Calculated & Viewed
Net Savings & Savings Rate Formula

• Net Savings = Total Inflow (Income) − Total Outflow (Expenses).
• Savings Rate = (Net Savings / Total Inflow) × 100.
• Where to see saved money: Home Dashboard Hero Card, Accounts Balances overview, Reports cumulative summary, and Financial X-Ray score.

2. Navigation Bar & Bottom Menu Buttons
🏠 Home Dashboard Tab
Your central overview. Shows net balance, quick stats, quick action buttons (+ Income, + Expense, + Transfer), and recent transactions.
🧾 Transactions Tab
Lists all financial logs. Features search bar, category filter pills, + Add Transaction button, pencil edit button on each item, and receipt photo attachments.
🏦 Accounts Tab
Manages bank accounts, cash wallets, and credit cards. Includes + Add Account button, transfer funds option, and dynamic balance counters.
🧭 Trackers Tab
Folder-like custom trackers (trips, projects, events) that open upon click. Includes + Add Tracker button, category `+` buttons, and export/reports in top right.
📊 Reports Tab
Interactive analytics with Cashflow Pulse, Category Spend Distribution, Person Summary, Account Balances, and Historical Balance Time Machine.
⚙️ Settings Tab
Access this App Guide, Appearance Studio (themes & blur), Device Permissions, Daily Check-in Reminder settings, and JSON/Excel data backup exports.
3. Action Buttons & Advanced Functions
➕ Add Transaction Button
Opens the transaction modal. Allows selecting account/wallet, income/expense/transfer type, amount, category, date, and attaching receipt photos.
🧾 Visual Receipt Cards & ✏️ Edit
Saving or tapping any transaction opens a visual Receipt Card preview with a top-right ✏️ edit button for instant modifications.
📂 Tracker Folders & Time Machine 2.0
Tap any Custom Tracker card to open its full Folder View with linked transactions, reports, and top-right Export. Use Time Machine to travel to past dates, forecast future wealth, or compare two dates side-by-side.
🛠️ Customizable Tools Hub & Themes Studio
In the More page, pin or move utilities between Tools Hub and All Tools, and customize 10 themes, 10 glass UIs, 10 animations, frosted blur, and 3D mesh flow.
🔔 Daily Check-in & Save Schedule
Configure exact daily check-in and monthly backup notification schedules offline with instant confirmation alerts.
